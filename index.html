♔ The Sacred Chronicles ♔

Scroll I — The Age of Origins

The Prophecy of the Bengere Kingdom

Long before the Wi-Fi routers were forged, deep within the legendary walls of the Bengere Kingdom, ancient elders gathered around a blazing fire and whispered of a warrior who would change history—mostly by showing up 45 minutes late and still acting like the main character.

That warrior was Sinaf. Scholars say when he was born, the skies over Bengere turned gold, three dragons bowed their heads, and a royal messenger immediately yelled, "Bro's aura is already too high, lock the gates."

Scroll II — Year of the Empty Fridge

The Great Siege of the Midnight Feast

Dark times fell upon the Bengere Kingdom when the royal kitchen ran low on snacks at 1:47 AM. Lesser knights surrendered to hunger. Not Sinaf. Clad in the sacred Armor of Sweats and armed with nothing but pure confidence, he marched down the shadowed hallway without making a single floorboard creak.

Against impossible odds—and two suspicious relatives guarding the living room—Lord Sinaf secured the final plate of food and vanished back into his fortress before anyone realized the treasure was gone. Bards still sing songs of his stealth.

Scroll III — The Battle of "5 More Minutes"

How Sinaf Defeated the Dark Lord of Alarms

Seven times the cursed bell of 7:00 AM rang across the towers of Bengere. Seven times it tried to drag the Commander from his royal chambers. Seven times Sinaf struck it down with a single blind swing of his right hand, muttering the ancient spell: "I'm literally getting up right now."

Historians confirm he did not get up until 11:30 AM, yet somehow arrived at the gathering looking fresher than everyone who woke up at dawn. Truly, the magic of Bengere works in mysterious ways.
